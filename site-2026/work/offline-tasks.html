Case study 16 · Public sector, Uruguay

An offline-first task manager for a prison in Uruguay

I built this on my own: a task manager for a prison in Uruguay. Inside there is no internet, so the app is offline-first. It syncs at an office outside with a connection, works fully offline inside, and syncs everything back on the way out.

Industry
Public sector, Uruguay
Role
Solo: design, build, delivery
Team
Just me
Stack
Flutter · offline-first

My role and responsibilities

  • Everything: requirements with the client, design, implementation and delivery.

Key decisions

  • Offline-first from day one. The work happens where there is no signal.
  • Sync only at the outside office. Download tasks and upload changes where there is internet.
  • Store evidence on the device. Photos and audio stay on the phone until the next sync.

What we built

  • Task tracking offline. Progress updates with no connection.
  • Photo and audio capture. As evidence for each task.
  • New tasks offline. Created inside, synced later.
  • Sync. One consistent picture at the office after every visit.

The hard part

Offline-first is a data problem: knowing what changed while offline and bringing it back consistently.

What I learned

Design for no connection first, and good connections become a bonus.
