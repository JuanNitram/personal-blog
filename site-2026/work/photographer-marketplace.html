Case study 13 · Photography marketplace

A marketplace that connects photographers and clients

An app that connected photographers with the people who need them: find one nearby, message them, get the photos and pay, all in one place. I led the team and the project.

Industry
Creative services
Role
Tech lead and project manager, also writing code
Team
Led a team of 3–4 developers
Key parts
Map · chat · uploads · payments
Built
By hand, pre-AI

My role and responsibilities

  • Led a small team of 3–4 developers: assigning work, reviewing code and unblocking people.
  • Acted as project manager: planning, scope, priorities and day-to-day communication with the client.
  • Owned the architecture and the main technical decisions.
  • Stayed hands-on, implementing core features myself.

Key decisions

  • Organize discovery around location. The most useful filter for finding a photographer is where they are.
  • Keep the whole job in one app. Search, chat, delivery and payment connected, so the same job is tracked from first message to last payment.

What we built

  • Map search. Find photographers nearby.
  • Messaging. Agree on the job inside the app.
  • Photo uploads. Photographers deliver through the platform.
  • Payments. The deal closes where it started.

The hard part

Each feature is simple on its own; the product only works when they connect without gaps.

What I learned

Users don't see features. They see one job, start to finish.
