Case study 10 · Guestio

A marketplace for podcast guests.

Guestio gives podcasters visibility and lets people book them through the platform. I worked on the product, including payments with Stripe and the subscriptions behind Pro accounts.

Product
Podcast marketplace
Users
Podcasters · bookers · Pros
Payments
Stripe · subscriptions
Built
By hand, pre-AI
Context

Being found is half the job.

Good podcasters and guests are hard to find. Guestio's goal is to make them visible and let others book them directly on the platform: discovery, booking and payment in one flow.

What I built
  1. Marketplace featuresWorked on the features that put podcasters in front of the right people and let them be booked.
  2. Payments with StripeProcessed payments for bookings through Stripe.
  3. Pro subscriptionsSome users were Pro, so I worked with Stripe subscriptions: recurring billing and the access that comes with it.
  4. The rest of the productPlus a set of other features around profiles and bookings.
The hard part

Two kinds of money in one product.

A marketplace that takes one-off payments and also sells recurring subscriptions has to keep both straight: who paid for what, what a Pro is allowed to do, and what happens when a subscription changes. Getting those rules right in code mattered more than any single screen.
