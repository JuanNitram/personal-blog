Case study 10 · US agricultural association

Measurement sheets and grower discovery for an agricultural association

A US agricultural association received sheets full of product measurements with every sale. We built a manager for those sheets, and a discovery integration to find growers in the US who didn't have a management system yet.

Industry
Agriculture, United States
Role
Tech lead and project manager, also writing code
Team
Led a team of 3–4 developers
Extra
Market discovery integration

My role and responsibilities

  • Led a small team of 3–4 developers: assigning work, reviewing code and unblocking people.
  • Acted as project manager: planning, scope, priorities and day-to-day communication with the client.
  • Owned the architecture and the main technical decisions.
  • Stayed hands-on, implementing core features myself.

Key decisions

  • Replace loose files with one system. Measurements are easier to find, compare and trust in one place.
  • Use an external API for discovery. To map which growers could use a system like this next.

What we built

  • Sheet manager. For the measurement sheets of each sale.
  • Discovery integration. Finding US growers without a management system.

The hard part

Solving today's problem while also pointing at the next opportunity.

What I learned

Sometimes the most useful query is "who doesn't have this yet?"
