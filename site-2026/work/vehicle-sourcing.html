Case study 09 · Car dealer

A vehicle sourcing platform for a car dealer

A car dealer wanted to buy from private sellers before the competition did. I led the platform that pulled listings from several sources into one place, checked each vehicle, and handled the conversation with the seller.

Industry
Automotive
Role
Tech lead and project manager, also writing code
Team
Led a team of 3–4 developers
Integrations
Vehicle data API · Facebook · Carfax · Postmark
Built
By hand, pre-AI

My role and responsibilities

  • Led a small team of 3–4 developers: assigning work, reviewing code and unblocking people.
  • Acted as project manager: planning, scope, priorities and day-to-day communication with the client.
  • Owned the architecture and the main technical decisions.
  • Stayed hands-on, implementing core features myself.

Key decisions

  • One data model for many sources. Each source had its own shape and gaps; everything is normalized into one vehicle-and-seller model.
  • Check quality before contacting. Carfax checks each vehicle before an offer goes out.
  • Capture replies with inbound email. Postmark inbound email routes sellers' replies back into the platform, so each conversation stays with its vehicle instead of in someone's inbox.

What we built

  • Centralized listings. From an external private-market vehicle data API.
  • Facebook data. A second source of who sells which vehicle.
  • Carfax checks. Vehicle quality and history.
  • Two-way email flow. Offers out, replies back in.

The hard part

Turning messy external data into one consistent model, then connecting it with a quality check and a two-way email flow the dealer could actually work from.

What I learned

Integration work is business rules wearing a technical costume.
