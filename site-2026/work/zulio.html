Case study 12 · Zulio

Card fees, paid by the customer.

Zulio lets a business pass the card processing fee to the customer instead of absorbing it. If the purchase is $1,000, the customer pays the fee on top, say about $1,030, and the business keeps the full amount. I worked on the Flutter app, built around Stripe and Tap to Pay.

Product
Fee pass-through payments
Stack
Flutter · Stripe
Key piece
Stripe Tap to Pay
Hardware
External devices
Context

Who pays the fee?

Every card payment has a processing fee. Usually the business eats it. Zulio flips that: the fee is added to the customer's total, not split, so the business receives exactly the price of the sale.

What I built
  1. Flutter appThe payments app itself, built in Flutter.
  2. Stripe, deeplyA strong integration with Stripe across the app's features: charging, fees and the payment flow end to end.
  3. Tap to PayContactless card payments right on the phone with Stripe Tap to Pay.
  4. External devicesIntegrations with different external devices used to take payments.
The hard part

Money math has no margin for error.

Adding the fee on top sounds simple, but the numbers must be exactly right every time, and they have to match what Stripe charges, across phones and external devices. Payments is where I've spent the most time in my career, and Zulio is one of the reasons why.
