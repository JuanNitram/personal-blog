Case study 09 · Vin Trackers

Finding cars before anyone else.

A car dealer wanted to buy from private sellers before the competition did. I helped build the platform that pulled listings from several sources into one place, checked each vehicle, and handled the conversation with the seller.

Product
Vehicle sourcing platform
Client
Vin Trackers (car dealer)
Key parts
Data APIs · Carfax · Postmark
Built
By hand, pre-AI

Private sellers are everywhere. That's the problem.

Cars sold by private owners are spread across many places. Vin Trackers, a car dealer, needed one view of all of them: what is for sale, who is selling it, and whether it is worth buying, so their team could contact the seller fast.

How it worked

What I built

  1. Centralized listings. Integrated an external API with private-market vehicle sales data, and brought it all into one place.
  2. Facebook data. Collected listings from Facebook to learn who was selling which vehicles, adding a second source to the same view.
  3. Carfax checks. Integrated Carfax to judge the quality and history of each vehicle before making an offer.
  4. Inbound email with Postmark. Used Postmark's inbound email feature to capture sellers' replies to purchase offers sent from the platform, so each conversation stayed with its vehicle instead of in someone's inbox.

Many sources, one source of truth.

Each source had its own shape and its own gaps. The real work was turning messy external data into one consistent model of a vehicle and its seller, then connecting it with a quality check and a two-way email flow the dealer could actually work from.
