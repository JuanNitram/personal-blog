Case study 14 · Childcare staffing company

Shift booking for nannies, with an admin calendar at the center

A platform where families hire nannies by shift. I led the work for all three sides (clients, nannies and admins) and on its centerpiece: an admin calendar that shows every shift at a glance.

Industry
Childcare
Role
Tech lead and project manager, also writing code
Team
Led a team of 3–4 developers
Users
Clients · nannies · admins
Built
By hand, pre-AI

My role and responsibilities

  • Led a small team of 3–4 developers: assigning work, reviewing code and unblocking people.
  • Acted as project manager: planning, scope, priorities and day-to-day communication with the client.
  • Owned the architecture and the main technical decisions.
  • Stayed hands-on, implementing core features myself.

Key decisions

  • Model everything around shifts. Families need someone for a specific time slot, not "in general".
  • Separate access for each role. Clients, nannies and admins each see what they need.
  • Make the admin calendar the centerpiece. Past, current and upcoming shifts, plus the type of each, readable in seconds.

What we built

  • Shift booking. By time slot.
  • Role-based access. For clients, nannies and admins.
  • Admin calendar. Every shift on one screen.
  • Data processing. Keeping shifts, people and bookings consistent.

The hard part

Customers never see the admin side, but the business lives in it.

What I learned

Good admin tools are product too. A calendar is the simplest way to make time visible.
