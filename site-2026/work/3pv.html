Case study 15 · 3pv

Campus events, one QR code away.

3pv was a ticketing app for universities. Students signed up, bought tickets to university events, and got in with a QR code. We built it with Ionic.

Product
University ticketing
Users
Students · universities
Stack
Ionic
Built
By hand, pre-AI

Paper tickets don't scale to a campus.

Universities run lots of events, and managing who bought a ticket and who got in by hand is slow and easy to cheat. 3pv moved the whole thing to the students' phones.

The flow

What I built

  1. Student accounts. Students register in the app with their university.
  2. Ticket purchase. Students buy tickets to university events directly from their phone.
  3. QR tickets. Each ticket is a QR code, used as the ticketing system at the event entrance.

Where my Ionic story started.

3pv was one of my first serious Ionic projects. What I learned there, one codebase reaching phones fast, came back later when we built Sermonly's mobile app.
