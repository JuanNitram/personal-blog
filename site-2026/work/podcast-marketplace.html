Case study 11 · Podcast marketplace

A marketplace for booking podcast guests

A marketplace that gives podcasters visibility and lets people book them through the platform. I led the work, including payments with Stripe and the subscriptions behind Pro accounts.

Industry
Podcasting
Role
Tech lead and project manager, also writing code
Team
Led a team of 3–4 developers
Payments
Stripe · subscriptions

My role and responsibilities

  • Led a small team of 3–4 developers: assigning work, reviewing code and unblocking people.
  • Acted as project manager: planning, scope, priorities and day-to-day communication with the client.
  • Owned the architecture and the main technical decisions.
  • Stayed hands-on, implementing core features myself.

Key decisions

  • One-off payments for bookings. Processed with Stripe.
  • Subscriptions for Pro users. Recurring billing and the access that comes with it.
  • Keep both money flows apart in the model. Who paid for what, and what a Pro can do, must never get mixed up.

What we built

  • Marketplace features. Discovery and booking.
  • Stripe payments. For bookings.
  • Pro subscriptions. With Stripe.

The hard part

A marketplace with one-off payments and recurring subscriptions has to keep both straight, including what happens when a subscription changes.

What I learned

Payments code is where business rules have to be exactly right.
