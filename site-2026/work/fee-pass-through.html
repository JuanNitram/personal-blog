Case study 12 · Payments startup

A Flutter payments app that passes card fees to the customer

A payments app that lets a business pass the card processing fee to the customer instead of absorbing it. On a $1,000 sale, the customer pays the fee on top, roughly $1,030, and the business keeps the full amount. I led the Flutter app, built around Stripe and Tap to Pay.

Industry
Payments
Role
Tech lead and project manager, also writing code
Team
Led a team of 3–4 developers
Stack
Flutter · Stripe · Tap to Pay
Hardware
External payment devices

My role and responsibilities

  • Led a small team of 3–4 developers: assigning work, reviewing code and unblocking people.
  • Acted as project manager: planning, scope, priorities and day-to-day communication with the client.
  • Owned the architecture and the main technical decisions.
  • Stayed hands-on, implementing core features myself.

Key decisions

  • Add the fee on top, never split it. The business receives exactly the sale price.
  • Use Stripe Tap to Pay. Contactless card payments right on the phone.
  • Support external devices. Integrations with different devices used to take payments.

What we built

  • Flutter app. The payments app itself.
  • Deep Stripe integration. Charging, fees and the payment flow end to end.
  • Tap to Pay. On the phone.

The hard part

The numbers must be exactly right every time and match what Stripe charges, across phones and external devices.

What I learned

In payments, "almost right" is just wrong.
