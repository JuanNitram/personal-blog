Case study 17 · Small businesses

Booking for barbershops through a WhatsApp bot

A small booking system for barbershops and similar businesses. What makes it different is a WhatsApp bot: customers check free days and book a time without leaving the chat. Simple, and it works.

Industry
Small businesses
Role
Solo
Team
Just me
Key piece
WhatsApp bot

My role and responsibilities

  • Everything, end to end: a deliberately small project.

Key decisions

  • Meet customers where they already are. They book through messages anyway, so the bot lives in WhatsApp.
  • Keep the scope small. Agenda, availability and booking, nothing more.

What we built

  • Business agenda. Calendar and time slots.
  • WhatsApp bot. See free days, book and adjust appointments.

The hard part

Making a conversation feel like a booking form without the form.

What I learned

The best interface is the one your customers already open every day.
