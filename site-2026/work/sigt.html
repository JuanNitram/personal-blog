Case study 16 · Sigt

A task manager that works behind prison walls.

I built Sigt on my own: a task manager for the Punta Rieles prison in Uruguay. Inside there is no internet, so the app is offline-first. It syncs at an office outside with a connection, works fully offline inside, and syncs everything back on the way out.

Product
Offline-first task manager
Client
Punta Rieles prison, Uruguay
Stack
Flutter · mobile
Built
Solo, by hand

No signal where the work happens.

Staff at Punta Rieles need to track tasks and their progress, with evidence. But inside the prison there is no internet access. The only connection is at an office outside. A normal online app would be useless exactly where it's needed.

Sync outside. Work inside. Sync again.

  1. Sync at the office. With internet at the outside office, the app pulls the latest tasks and pushes everything recorded since the last visit.
  2. Work fully offline. Inside, staff keep tracking progress on tasks with no connection at all.
  3. Evidence on the spot. Take photos and record audio as part of a task, stored on the device until the next sync.
  4. New tasks, offline too. Create new tasks inside the prison; they join the shared data once the phone syncs again.

Offline-first is a data problem.

Making screens work without internet is the easy part. The real challenge is the data: storing tasks, photos and audio safely on the phone, knowing what changed while offline, and bringing it back so the office sees one consistent picture after every sync.
