Case study 17 · Mi Reserva

Book a haircut on WhatsApp.

Mi Reserva is a small booking system for barbershops and similar businesses. What makes it different is its WhatsApp bot: customers check free days and book a time without leaving the chat. Simple, and it works.

Product
Booking for small businesses
Users
Barbershops · their customers
Key piece
WhatsApp bot
Size
Mini project

Customers already live in WhatsApp.

Barbershops and similar businesses book most appointments through messages. Asking customers to download an app or open a website adds friction. So Mi Reserva meets them where they already are.

The flow

  1. Agenda for the business. A scheduling system where the business manages its calendar and time slots.
  2. WhatsApp bot. Customers talk to a bot on WhatsApp to manage their booking.
  3. Availability and booking. Through the chat they see available days, book a time and adjust their appointment.

Small scope, real use.

Mi Reserva is deliberately simple. The lesson was choosing the right channel over more features: meeting customers inside WhatsApp mattered more than any extra screen.
