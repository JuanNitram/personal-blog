Case study 13 · CAP · Creative Agent Partners

Find a photographer on the map.

For Creative Agent Partners (CAP) we designed an app that connected photographers with the people who need them: find one nearby, message them, get the photos, and pay, all in one place.

Product
Photographer marketplace
Client
Creative Agent Partners
Key parts
Map · chat · uploads · payments
Built
By hand, pre-AI
Context

The right photographer is usually nearby.

Finding a photographer often means searching many sites and sending messages into the void. The app put photographers and clients in the same place, organized around the most useful filter of all: where they are.

The flow
What I built
  1. Map searchFind photographers on a map, based on where they are.
  2. MessagingWrite to a photographer directly from the app to agree on the job.
  3. Photo uploadsPhotographers upload the photos they took, delivered through the platform.
  4. PaymentsProcess payments for the work, so the deal closes in the same place it started.
What I learned

A marketplace is a chain. Every link counts.

Each feature is simple on its own. The product only works when search, chat, delivery and payment connect without gaps, so the same job, people and photos are tracked from the first message to the last payment.
