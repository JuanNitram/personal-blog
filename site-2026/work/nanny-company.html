Case study 14 · Nanny Company

Every shift, on one calendar.

Nanny Company let families hire nannies by shift. I worked on the platform for all three sides (clients, nannies and admins) and on its centerpiece: an admin calendar that shows every shift at a glance.

Product
Nanny booking by shift
Users
Clients · nannies · admins
Centerpiece
Admin shift calendar
Built
By hand, pre-AI

Childcare runs on time slots.

Families don't hire a nanny "in general". They need someone for a specific shift. The platform let clients book nannies by time slot, and gave the business a way to run all of those shifts without spreadsheets.

Three sides

What I built

  1. Shift booking. Clients hire nannies by shift, for specific time slots.
  2. Access for each role. Separate experiences for clients, nannies and administrators, each seeing what they need.
  3. The admin calendar. The core admin feature: a calendar that clearly shows shifts that already happened, shifts happening now and shifts coming up, plus the type of each shift.
  4. Data processing. Processing behind the scenes to keep shifts, people and bookings consistent.

Good admin tools are product too.

Customers never see the admin side, but the business lives in it. Putting every shift on one readable calendar turned a pile of bookings into something a person can understand and act on in seconds.
