Case study 15 · University events

QR ticketing for university events

A ticketing app for universities. Students signed up, bought tickets to university events, and got in with a QR code. We built it with Ionic.

Industry
Education · events
Role
Tech lead and project manager, also writing code
Team
Led a team of 3–4 developers
Stack
Ionic
Built
By hand, pre-AI

My role and responsibilities

  • Led a small team of 3–4 developers: assigning work, reviewing code and unblocking people.
  • Acted as project manager: planning, scope, priorities and day-to-day communication with the client.
  • Owned the architecture and the main technical decisions.
  • Stayed hands-on, implementing core features myself.

Key decisions

  • Put the ticket on the student's phone. Paper tickets are slow to manage and easy to cheat.
  • Use QR codes as the ticket. Simple to scan at the door.
  • Build with Ionic. One codebase to reach phones fast.

What we built

  • Student accounts. Registration with their university.
  • Ticket purchase. From the phone.
  • QR tickets. Scanned at the entrance.

The hard part

Keeping the whole flow, from sign-up to entry, simple enough for a busy event door.

What I learned

One of my first serious Ionic projects; what I learned came back later in a desktop-to-mobile product.
