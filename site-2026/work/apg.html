Case study 10 · APG · American Pistachio Growers

Pistachio data, out of the spreadsheets.

American Pistachio Growers received sheets full of measurements for the pistachios being sold. We built a manager for those sheets, and a discovery integration to find growers in the US who didn't have a management system yet.

Product
Measurement sheet manager
Client
American Pistachio Growers
Extra
Grower discovery
Domain
Agriculture
Context

Every sale comes with a sheet.

Pistachios are sold with measurements, and those measurements arrived as sheets. Keeping them in files makes them hard to find, compare and trust. APG needed one place to manage them.

What we built
  1. Sheet managerA system to receive and manage the measurement sheets for the pistachios being sold, instead of loose files.
  2. Discovery integrationAn integration with an external API, built as discovery, to find which growers in the United States still had no management system.
Why it mattered

A tool, and a map of who needs it.

The sheet manager solved today's problem. The discovery work pointed at tomorrow's: which growers could use a system like this next. It's the same idea as the Tithely scrapers that checked which churches already had online giving: use data to find who still needs the product.
