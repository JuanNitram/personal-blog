Case study 03 · Tithely · Breeze Worship

Setlists for church bands, in Flutter.

Breeze Worship helps churches plan the songs played at their services and events. I worked on the mobile app in Flutter with BLoC and clean architecture, including a hard part: showing PDFs inside the app through a bridge to HTML, CSS and JavaScript. All written by hand.

Product
Worship setlist planning
Users
Church worship teams
Stack
Flutter · BLoC · WebView
Built
By hand, pre-AI
Context

Every service has a setlist.

Worship teams plan which songs they'll play at each event, in what order, with what notes, and they need the right chord and lyric sheets for every musician. Breeze Worship brings all of that into one app.

Features
  1. CCLI integrationConnects to CCLI, a very large song database, to pull chord sheets, lyric sheets and every other kind of sheet tied to a song.
  2. Setlist managementAdd, edit, move songs up and down, and attach notes to build the setlist for each event.
  3. MP3 uploadsUpload audio files so the team can listen and rehearse.
The hard parts

Clean architecture, and a bridge.

01

Flutter with BLoC, done cleanly

Built the app in Flutter with the BLoC pattern and a very clean architecture: UI, business logic and data kept apart. Getting that right was a challenge in itself.

02

PDFs inside a mobile app

Song sheets are PDFs, and they had to render well inside the app. We built a module that bridges Flutter with HTML, CSS and JavaScript to display them. Two runtimes talking to each other was the hardest piece of the project.

Hand-built bridge
What I learned

Architecture pays you back later.

A clean BLoC structure felt slow at first. It paid off the moment we had to plug in something as unusual as a web-based PDF viewer: the bridge had one clear place to live instead of leaking into every screen.
