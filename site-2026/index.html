Case studies

Through BuildOnline: Tithely and Rural King

Since 2020 I've worked as a contractor through BuildOnline, mostly for Tithely, a platform for churches. These are products I've designed, built and maintained there. I also worked on scrapers that checked church websites for giving buttons, to see which churches already had an online giving provider.

  • Sermonly: A Notion for churches, built from zero
    Designed and built solo: AI, wording and Bible Gateway integrations, Stripe subscriptions, ~8,000 users — on Electron, web and Ionic.
  • Media: One media library, everywhere
    Tithely's multimedia library and storage, plus the NPM package that opens it as a modal inside many Tithely products.
  • Breeze Worship: Setlists for church bands, in Flutter
    CCLI song sheets, MP3s and setlist editing — Flutter with BLoC, and a hand-built bridge to render PDFs with HTML/JS.
  • Analytics: Making signup data worth trusting
    Audited GA4, PostHog and GTM by hand, found three silent bugs, then shipped the production cutover — and watched it.
  • Billing: Annual billing, de-risked before it ships
    Caught where planning notes and code disagreed on subscription behavior, and found unticketed scope before the deadline did.
  • Quality: Test automation, started from scratch
    A new Playwright project from zero, Xray fixed so QA counts, containerized CI — built with AI, reviewed by me.
  • Rural King: A store scanner, rendered on the server
    A replacement for Rural King's in-store handheld app: scan products, manage stock, check other US stores and work through tasks — server-rendered screens inside native iOS and Android wrappers.

Client work

Most of these projects were written feature by feature by hand, before AI coding tools. That's where I learned to turn abstract business rules into real architecture, usually as tech lead: assigning work and connecting the client with the code.

  • Studeo: A podcast platform, and my first Kubernetes project
    Podcast management with feeds for Spotify, Apple and Google Podcasts, and my first hands-on Kubernetes project: moving production to autoscaling.
  • Vin Trackers: Finding cars before anyone else
    Private-market vehicle listings from an API and Facebook in one place, Carfax checks, and seller replies captured with Postmark inbound email.
  • APG: Pistachio data, out of the spreadsheets
    A manager for American Pistachio Growers' measurement sheets, plus a discovery integration to find US growers without a management system.
  • Guestio: A marketplace for podcast guests
    Visibility and bookings for podcasters, with Stripe payments and the subscriptions behind Pro accounts.
  • Zulio: Card fees, paid by the customer
    A Flutter payments app that adds the processing fee to the customer's total, with Stripe Tap to Pay and external devices.
  • CAP: Find a photographer on the map
    An app for Creative Agent Partners connecting photographers and clients: map search, messaging, photo uploads and payments.
  • Nanny Company: Every shift, on one calendar
    Book nannies by shift, with access for clients, nannies and admins — and an admin calendar showing every shift at a glance.
  • 3pv: Campus events, one QR code away
    A university ticketing app: students sign up, buy tickets to campus events and get in with a QR code.

Independent projects

  • Sigt: A task manager that works behind prison walls
    Built solo for the Punta Rieles prison: track tasks, take photos and record audio with no internet, then sync at the office outside.
  • Mi Reserva: Book a haircut on WhatsApp
    A booking system for barbershops where customers check free days and book a time through a WhatsApp bot.
