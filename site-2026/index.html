About me

I'm Juan, a software engineer from Montevideo, Uruguay. I've been working in the industry for about nine years. I started as a freelancer, went through agencies and product teams, and over time grew into leading teams.

Today I work as a Technical Lead at BuildOnline, where I lead a team of fifteen people. Before that I led smaller teams of three or four developers, often taking care of project management while still writing code alongside them.

What I enjoy most is the people side of building software. I like sharing what I know with my teammates and learning from them, working through problems together instead of alone, and making room for open, honest conversation. In my experience, good communication matters as much as the technical solution, and I try to bring both to every team I'm part of.

What I focus on

  • Building reliable products that are easy to maintain over time.
  • Helping teams work well together: clear priorities, shared knowledge and space to ask questions.
  • Being a bridge between what the business needs and what the team builds.
